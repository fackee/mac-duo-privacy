Mac Duo — Help & Contact

Getting Started

Mac Duo brings the iPhone Duo screen effect to your MacBook. When you close the lid, your screen content tilts and blurs in 3D depth, giving you a subtle, satisfying animation every time you step away.

System Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • MacBook with a built-in lid angle sensor (all modern MacBook Pro and MacBook Air models)
  • ~5 MB disk space

Installation

  1. Download Mac Duo from the App Store
  2. Open the app (it lives in your menu bar)
  3. Grant Screen Recording permission when prompted (required to capture the screen for the depth effect)
  4. Close your lid gently to see the effect in action

FAQ

The effect doesn't show up when I close the lid?

First, make sure Screen Recording permission is enabled: open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording and check that Mac Duo is toggled on. You may need to quit and reopen the app after enabling the permission.

“No lid angle sensor” message?

Mac Duo requires a MacBook with a built-in lid angle sensor. All modern MacBook Pro and MacBook Air models with a hinged display have this sensor. If you see this message on a supported model, try restarting the app or restarting your Mac.

If you're running on a desktop Mac (iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio), the lid sensor is not available — the app is designed for MacBook laptops only.

The screen stays dark after opening?

The overlay window should disappear automatically when you open the lid past the threshold angle. If it gets stuck, press Esc or click anywhere on the screen. If the problem persists, quit Mac Duo from the menu bar and reopen it.

Can I customize the effect?

Yes. Click the Mac Duo icon in your menu bar and choose Settings. You can adjust:

  • Blur radius and intensity
  • Depth / recession amount
  • Dim level
  • Start angle (when the effect kicks in)
  • Live preview to see your changes in real time

Does Mac Duo drain battery?

Minimal. The app only polls the lid sensor a few times per second, and the screen effect runs only while the lid is actively closing. When your Mac is open and you're using it normally, the app is nearly idle. You can also set a longer poll interval in Settings to save even more power.

Is my screen data being recorded or sent anywhere?

No. Mac Duo captures the screen only to render the depth effect, and the capture stays in memory only — it is never saved to disk, never transmitted, and never leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I uninstall Mac Duo?

Simply drag Mac Duo from your Applications folder to the Trash, or use Launchpad to delete it. All preferences are stored in system defaults and will be removed automatically when you uninstall the app.

Does it work with external displays?

Mac Duo shows the depth effect on your built-in display only. External displays are not affected and continue working normally when the lid is closed.
