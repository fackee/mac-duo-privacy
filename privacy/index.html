Privacy Policy

Mac Duo — Last updated: September 15, 2026

No Data Collection

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Mac Duo handles your information. The short version is: Mac Duo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from your device.

Bottom line: Everything Mac Duo does stays on your Mac. No data ever leaves your device.

What Mac Duo Does

Mac Duo creates a subtle depth and blur effect on your screen when you close your MacBook lid. It works entirely on your device using:

  • Lid angle sensor — reads the built-in hinge angle sensor to detect when the lid is closing. This data is processed locally and never stored or transmitted.
  • Screen capture — takes a snapshot of your screen to render the depth effect. The capture stays in memory only and is discarded immediately after use. Nothing is saved to disk or sent anywhere.
  • Local preferences — settings like blur strength, depth amount, and threshold angle are stored on your Mac using the system defaults database (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

Mac Duo does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, contact details)
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Crash reports or performance metrics
  • Location data
  • Photos, files, or other content from your device
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any third-party SDKs or tracking frameworks

iCloud and Sync

Mac Duo does not use iCloud, does not sync data across devices, and does not use any cloud services. Your preferences stay local to your Mac.

Children's Privacy

Mac Duo is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all, no child-specific data is ever gathered.

Your Rights

Because Mac Duo does not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. Your preferences are stored locally on your Mac and can be removed by deleting the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how Mac Duo handles data in a future update, we will update this policy and clearly note the changes. We will never add data collection without your explicit consent.
